How to Play

  1. Pick a category, then pick a grade level to reveal that question. Higher grades are worth more points (1st: 100, 2nd: 200, 3rd: 300, 4th: 500, 5th: 1000).
  2. Read the question out loud and let both teams answer.
  3. Click Reveal Answer to show the correct answer.
  4. Click Team 1 and/or Team 2 to award that team the points — both teams can score on the same question. Click a team again to undo it.
  5. Click Back to Categories to continue. Once all 20 questions are answered, the final scores decide the winner.
  6. Use the + / − buttons on either scoreboard to adjust a team's score manually at any time.
  7. Use Reset Game below to clear all scores and start over.

Cheats

You may choose ONE classmate to help with a question:

  1. Peek — Look at your classmate's answer, then decide whether to use your own answer or theirs.
  2. Copy — You MUST use your classmate's answer. No changing your mind.
  3. Save — You answer normally. If you're wrong but your classmate answered correctly, you're saved and stay in the game. If both are wrong, you're out.
