Setup

  • Bring your two teams to the front.
  • Before the game starts, each team sets a fixed contestant order. This order cannot change once the game begins.

Starting a Round

  • The next contestant in each team's order comes up.
  • Each contestant picks one teammate to be their "student" for the round.
  • A student cannot be picked two rounds in a row. A student who sat out the previous round is eligible again.
  • The two contestants play rock-paper-scissors. The winner chooses the category and the grade level of the question.

Answering

  • Both teams answer the question silently and independently.
  • The contestant and their student answer together, privately, as a pair. Both answers are revealed at the end of the round.
  • Teammates who are not the contestant or student may chat privately for fun, but cannot influence the round.

Cheats

Each team may use cheats only with the one classmate (student) chosen for that round. The total number of cheats is shared across the entire team.

CheatUsesHow it works
Peek 3 Look at your student's answer, then choose to keep your own answer or switch to theirs. Once a Peek is used, no one else on the team may react or give hints.
Copy 3 You must use your student's answer as-is. No changing your mind after.
Save 2 Answer normally. If you get the question wrong, you may choose to use a Save: if your student answered correctly, you get the points instead of zero. If your student was also wrong, you still get zero.

Scoring

  • Points are awarded per correct answer, scaled by grade level.
  • The team with the most points at the end of the game wins.
  • 5th Grade — 500 points
  • 4th Grade — 400 points
  • 3rd Grade — 300 points
  • 2nd Grade — 200 points
  • 1st Grade — 100 points

Tie-Breaker

If the game ends in a tie or is very close, use a multiplication table round to decide the winner. Time limit: 1 minute to complete. Whoever has the most correct answers wins all the points.

Using This App

  • Pick a category, then pick a grade level to reveal that question.
  • Read the question out loud and let both teams answer.
  • Click Reveal Answer to show the correct answer.
  • Click Team 1 and/or Team 2 to award that team the points — both teams can score on the same question. Click a team again to undo it.
  • Click Back to Categories to continue. Once all 20 questions are answered, the final scores decide the winner.
  • Use the + / − buttons on either scoreboard to adjust a team's score manually, and the Peek/Copy/Save − buttons to track cheat uses.
  • Use Reset Game below to clear all scores and cheat counts and start over.
